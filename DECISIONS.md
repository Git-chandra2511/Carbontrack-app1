# DECISIONS

## DP1 – The Nudge
When the weekly target is crossed, the app gives a **positive congratulatory nudge** to appreciate the user’s progress. This encourages them to continue their sustainable habits without making them feel pressured or ashamed.

## DP2 – Absurd Input
The app **validates user input** and flags unrealistic values, such as a 500,000 km car trip. This helps prevent incorrect data from affecting the app’s calculations and results.

## DP3 – The Week
The app considers a week as a **fixed seven-day period** and displays progress as the week goes on. Mid-week progress helps users see how much of their target they have completed and whether they are staying on track.
